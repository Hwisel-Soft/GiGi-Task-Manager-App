# Privacy Policy for GiGi

**Last updated: October 10, 2026**

## Overview

GiGi does not collect, store, or share any personal data with the developer or any third party.

## Data Storage

All tasks and app data you create in GiGi are stored:

- **On your device** using local storage
- **In your personal iCloud account** if you have iCloud enabled

Your iCloud data is managed entirely by Apple and governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/). The developer has no access to your iCloud data.

## Data We Do Not Collect

- No personal information
- No usage analytics
- No crash reports
- No location data
- No advertising identifiers
- No contacts or calendar data beyond what you explicitly book through the app

## Calendar Access

GiGi requests access to your calendar solely to add tasks you book within the app. Calendar data is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

## Notifications

GiGi uses local notifications for morning briefs and weekly stats. These are generated entirely on your device and are not transmitted anywhere.

## Siri Integration (iOS)

GiGi integrates with Siri to allow voice-based task creation. This feature uses Apple's SiriKit and is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/).
